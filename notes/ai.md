**Research Notes – Artificial Intelligence (AI)**

---

### 1. Core Definition
- **AI** = computational systems that can perform tasks traditionally requiring human intelligence (learning, reasoning, problem‑solving, perception, language understanding, decision‑making).
- Emerges from **engineering, mathematics, and computer science**; draws on disciplines such as statistics, linguistics, neuroscience, philosophy, and psychology.
- Distinguishes **Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)** – task‑specific competence – from **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)** (human‑level, adaptive) and **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** (beyond human capability).

### 2. Major Goals & Vision
| Goal | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)** | Machine that can learn, adapt, and reason across a wide range of tasks like a human. Not yet realized. |
| **Intelligent Agent** | Autonomous system that perceives environment, selects actions to maximize defined goals. |
| **Recursive Self‑Improvement** | Ability of an AI to iteratively improve its own architecture/algorithms. |
| **Planning & Decision‑Making** | Long‑term strategy formulation in dynamic environments. |
| **Computer Vision** | Interpreting visual data (e.g., image classification, object detection). |
| **General Game Playing** | Mastery of diverse games without task‑specific re‑programming. |
| **Knowledge Representation** | Structured encoding of facts, rules, and relationships for inference. |
| **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** | Understanding and generating human language (chatbots, translation). |
| **Robotics** | Embodied AI that interacts physically with the world. |
| **AI Safety** | Ensuring alignment with human values, preventing harmful outcomes (especially for AGI/ASI). |

### 3. Primary Approaches & Techniques
| Category | Representative Methods |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Machine Learning (ML)** | Supervised/unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning. |
| **Deep Learning** | Multi‑layer neural networks (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers). |
| **Symbolic AI** | Rule‑based systems, logic programming, knowledge graphs. |
| **Bayesian Networks** | Probabilistic reasoning and inference. |
| **Evolutionary Algorithms** | Genetic programming, neuro‑evolution. |
| **Neuro‑Symbolic AI** | Hybrid of connectionist (neural) and symbolic reasoning. |
| **Data Mining** | Extracting patterns from large datasets. |
| **Systems Integration / Open‑Source** | Combining multiple AI components; community‑driven toolkits. |
| **AI Data Centers** | Specialized hardware (GPUs, TPUs) and infrastructure for large‑scale training. |

### 4. Application Domains
- **Arts & Creativity** – Music composition, visual art generation.
- **Higher Education** – Personalized learning pathways, virtual assistants, learning analytics, automated grading, content creation, admissions screening (EDUCAUSE).
- **Healthcare** – Diagnostic assistance, personalized treatment planning, surgical robotics.
- **Transportation** – Autonomous vehicles, intelligent traffic management.
- **Finance & Business** – Algorithmic trading, fraud detection, economic forecasting, cost reduction, productivity gains.
- **Industry & Manufacturing** – Predictive maintenance, process optimization.
- **Consumer Services** – Chatbots, recommendation engines, language translation.

### 5. Roles & Workforce Implications
- **AI Engineers** – Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML solutions; focus on efficiency, cost‑reduction, and business impact.
- **Research Scientists** – Advance core algorithms, create new AI capabilities, address safety/ethics.
- **Business Intelligence Developers** – Organize, analyze, and visualize data; build decision‑support dashboards.
- **Computer Vision Engineers** – Build systems that interpret visual inputs (e.g., QR‑code scanning, object detection).

### 6. Ethical, Safety, & Existential Concerns
- **AGI/ASI Risks** – Potential for autonomous, self‑improving systems to act beyond human control; some researchers assign non‑negligible probability to catastrophic outcomes (e.g., human extinction).
- **Safety Research** – Emphasis on alignment, robustness, transparency, and governance to mitigate misuse or unintended behavior.
